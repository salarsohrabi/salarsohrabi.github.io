Engineering profile

From registers and interrupts to complete software platforms.

My work sits at the boundary between hardware and software: architecture, bring-up, real-time behavior, operating systems, integration and the practical debugging needed to make complex systems dependable.

I have delivered embedded and automotive software across production environments including Volvo Cars, Volvo Trucks and Renault Group, with experience spanning Classic/Adaptive AUTOSAR, QNX, Embedded Linux, Android/AOSP, NVIDIA automotive compute and MCU-based real-time systems.

I am most effective where problems cross layers—boot, drivers, middleware, application behavior, hardware interaction, timing, test infrastructure and system integration—rather than living inside a single box.
