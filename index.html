Engineering profile

Systems thinking grounded in hands-on implementation.

My strongest work is where hardware and software meet: timing, peripherals, drivers, operating systems, communication, integration and the debugging needed to turn complex platforms into dependable products.

Recent automotive work covers AURIX/RTA-OS/AUTOSAR, QNX/DriveOS on NVIDIA Orin/Xavier, HIL and automated testing, AOSP platform software, and Adaptive AUTOSAR/SOME-IP.

Earlier product work adds deep context in embedded Linux/BSP, GNSS/RTK, multilayer PCB, FPGA/DSP systems, industrial control, medical devices, motion systems and power electronics.
