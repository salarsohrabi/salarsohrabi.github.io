Detailed engineering profile

Embedded systems across the whole stack.

I am a senior embedded software and hardware engineer with 15+ years of stated experience across automotive platforms, real-time firmware, embedded Linux, QNX, GNSS/RTK, IoT, medical devices, industrial control and power electronics. My strongest work sits at the boundary between hardware and software—where timing, interfaces, operating systems, physical signals and production constraints have to work together.

Capability map

The profile is broad, but the center of gravity is clear.

The recurring theme is systems engineering close to hardware: real-time behavior, platform software, interfaces, diagnostics, integration and the practical debugging needed to make embedded products dependable.

01 · Firmware

MCU & real-time software

Hardware-near C/C++ work on automotive and industrial controllers, including real-time execution and peripheral integration.

  • AURIX TC2/TC3 project evidence, including TC29/TC36/TC39 references.
  • GTM, ADC, MPU, interrupts, timing and target-side debugging.
  • RTA-OS project work; broader RTOS inventory includes FreeRTOS, uC/OS and mbedOS.
  • STM32/LPC/Cortex-M based control products across earlier industrial work.
02 · Platform

Embedded Linux, BSP & bring-up

Platform work spanning boot, kernel, device description, drivers, root filesystem, connectivity and board-level integration.

  • Yocto and Buildroot based embedded Linux systems.
  • U-Boot, kernel customization, Device Tree and BSP integration.
  • Linux driver work for Bluetooth, high-speed USB and 4G WWAN in GNSS products.
  • Board bring-up and hardware/software diagnosis on Cortex-A class systems.
03 · Automotive

Classic & Adaptive AUTOSAR

Automotive development across MCU firmware, platform integration, communication and safety-related software processes.

  • AUTOSAR Classic with RTA-OS and DaVinci configuration in Volvo Trucks work.
  • Adaptive AUTOSAR, FIDL and SOME/IP in Renault project descriptions.
  • ISO 26262-related development, MISRA/Polyspace, requirements and FMEA contributions.
  • CAN/CANoe/CANalyzer/Kvaser tooling and HIL/SIL-oriented verification.
04 · Modern systems

QNX, C++ & heterogeneous compute

Production-oriented C++ integration on automotive compute platforms and POSIX-style embedded environments.

  • NVIDIA Orin/Xavier with DriveOS/QNX.
  • IPC, UDP/TCP and test-application integration.
  • Robot Framework/HIL workflows, Jenkins and Docker.
  • Cross-layer debugging between application, OS, services and hardware-facing components.
05 · Hardware

PCB, FPGA, DSP & physical systems

Earlier engineering work includes complete hardware/software products rather than software-only integration.

  • Multilayer and high-speed board design, including 6/10/12-layer examples.
  • Zynq, Spartan-6 and wider FPGA/DSP inventories.
  • Motion control, industrial I/O, sensors, displays and noisy electrical environments.
  • Power electronics, PFC, chargers, electronic loads and high-voltage PWM integration.
06 · Delivery

Testing, integration & technical leadership

Hands-on delivery across implementation, automated validation, system integration and small-team technical leadership.

  • Robot Framework, Cantata, GoogleTest and hardware-target testing.
  • Lauterbach/T32, GDB, Kvaser and Vector tooling.
  • Git/Gerrit/Bitbucket, Jenkins, Docker and CI/CD workflows.
  • Historical team leadership in GNSS, smart-home and product-development contexts.
